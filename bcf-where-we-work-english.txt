--- Projects — Erbil ---

[Food Security]
2021 — Food distributions across Erbil: Korek parcels for 350 households, Galiawa food for 6,817 households in Hasan Sham U2/U3 and Khazir, and Qurbani meat for 14,021 households in the city and its camps.
2022 — The hot-meal programme served 694,362 meals in Erbil, alongside food distributions continuing through partner projects.
2023 — Qurbani meat reached 1,481 families, with regular food and non-food assistance continuing across Erbil and the camps.
2024 — The Ramadan overview recorded 14,943 food parcels in Erbil; other Ramadan and food projects ran separately.
Scope: Annual report and website Ramadan overviews cover different project scopes.
2026 — Ramadan iftar at Erbil Park for 1,000 people daily, 500 Turaq food baskets, food for families of martyrs, Eid food baskets and camp food and flour assistance.
2007 — Food parcels reached 700 families across four Erbil neighbourhoods, and 500 martyrs' families in Sarwaran received additional Eid support.
2015 — Monthly food aid reached 12,741 refugees in Dibaga, Khabat and Koya, and 1,298 tonnes were distributed over the year.

[Education & Development]
2020 — The 450 school-chairs project covered Halgurd Basic School in Erbil together with schools under the Sulaymaniyah and Penjwen directorates.
2022 — Fifty of the 203 schools in the renovation programme were in Erbil.
2024 — Fifteen schools renovated in Erbil, and a solar-electricity pilot installed at ten Erbil schools.
2025 — The Orphans' Educational Project was officially announced in Erbil and operates across BCF offices and Kurdistan.
2026 — First participation in the Kurdish Book Fair, education and awareness initiatives, and a new Capacity Development Center for Orphans and Widows announced in Erbil.
2008 — A regional school-supplies campaign reached 7,700 pupils, including 17 schools in Erbil, Shaqlawa and the governorate's displacement camps.
2021 — A USD 3.5 million college-building project was announced at Salahaddin University.
2023 — Education activities recorded 39,745 beneficiaries in Erbil.
Scope: Livelihood activities recorded 40,054 beneficiaries in the same year; education and livelihood stay separate records.

[Health]
2020 — COVID-19 medical assistance delivered to Central Erbil Hospital as part of regional hospital support.
2024 — The Center of Excellence for Complex Care was active, and Erbil hospitals also received patients through the mobility-service project.
2026 — The memorandum for the Kurdistan Center of Excellence for Complex Care was renewed, and a mammography support project announced for the Erbil breast-disease centre.
2006 — BCF supplied health facilities to hospitals in Erbil, benefiting 2,144 people in its first documented year.
2015 — The Harsham IDP Camp Health Center opened on 28 February to provide care inside the camp.
2024 — Health activities recorded 13,597 beneficiaries in Erbil.

[Water & Sanitation]
2021 — Emergency water response delivered 65,864,000 litres through 3,261 tanker runs to 65,220 families across Erbil Governorate.
2022 — Well drilling and repair, pumps and generators in Kawrgosk, Qushtapa, Kasnazan-area villages and Ruvia, with related countryside water systems.
2024 — WASH activities reached 9,508 families in Erbil Governorate, representing 48,180 people.
2015 — BCF delivered 120,000 litres of clean water daily to Erbil's Qalai Nwe and Qarabu neighbourhoods.

[Shelter & Housing]
2015 — BCF built 304 caravan homes in Bahirka Camp, and 251 displaced families were resettled there in September.
Scope: Recorded in the cumulative 2005-2024 report.
2019 — Khazir camp shelter support, including large tent projects with LDS Charities and Catholic Teufen.
2021 — Two major flood responses in Erbil: 751 households assisted in October and 2,905 households in December.
2023 — BCF responded again to flood-affected families in Erbil after heavy rains.
2024 — The foundation stone was laid for BCF City in Qushtapa, and the shared Erbil/Soran martyrs' housing distribution took place.
Scope: 540 units project-wide; the Erbil-vs-Soran split is not published in the reviewed source.
2025 — The first phase of BCF City moved into implementation in Qushtapa, including 180 houses for poor and low-income families.
2011 — BCF supplied 90 complete bedroom sets to the Erbil Social Welfare Directorate.
2024 — Household-support records reached 24,796 people in Erbil and shelter records 2,682.
Scope: Two separate records. The report never sums household support and shelter.

[Disability Support]
2015 — The mobility-aid project launched with LDS Charities and continued in later years.
2021 — 389 people in Erbil received wheelchairs or other mobility aids.
2022 — 326 beneficiaries recorded in Erbil.
2026 — Wheelchairs and other disability support continued: ten wheelchairs distributed in March, with further support in April.
2009 — Forty-two hearing aids were provided to children with hearing loss in Erbil.
2015 — On World Autism Awareness Day, BCF presented gifts to 150 children with autism in Erbil.
2024 — The autism centre in Erbil reports annual support for 60 to 90 children.

[Camp Management]
2021 — BCF managed nine IDP and refugee camps plus one host-community camp in Erbil Governorate; clothing, blanket and household-item projects supported Harsham, Hasan Sham, Bahirka, Debaga and others.
2021-26 — Recurring food, NFI, winter, health and camp-support projects operated in Bahirka, Hasan Sham, Khazir, Debaga, Qushtapa, Basirma, Darashakran and Harsham.
2023 — Seven camps managed in Erbil, and more than 300,000 Iranian pilgrims temporarily accommodated in Sami Abdulrahman Park with food, water and health services.
2024 — The Warm Winter programme distributed heaters to schools in Erbil; camp coordination remained one of BCF's largest sectors.
2026 — Food, cash, clothing and disability assistance continued in Baharka, Hasan Sham, Khazir and Debaga camps, and a World Refugee Day event was held in Erbil.
2011 — BCF assisted 100 families in the Kawa, Jazhnekan, Sebiran and Azadi camps in Koya.
2015 — BCF took over the administration of Harsham camp in January, following approval by UNHCR and the Erbil Refugee Council.

[Cash & Orphans]
2012 — Orphan sponsorship — the Kurdistan project — began and later expanded across BCF offices.
2021 — Erbil joined the academic-excellence programme honouring top Grade 12 orphan students.
2025 — The Azizan-Kurdistan March stage reached 802 orphans in Erbil, and the educational project launched in the city.
2025-26 — Nujin operates in Erbil.
Scope: 15 families in 2025 and 12 in 2026 are project-wide totals across all offices, not Erbil counts.
2026 — The Capacity Development Center for Orphans and Widows was announced, and food, clothing and family assistance continued.
2010 — Eighty-one children in Erbil's orphanages received Eid gifts of USD 100 each.
2021 — The UAE-supported OrphanCare project recorded 3,195 children in Erbil.
2024 — Cash assistance reached 1,027 Erbil families, representing 8,759 people.
