--- Sector: Food Security ---

Food Security
Food security is one of BCF's core humanitarian sectors. Food assistance reaches vulnerable families, internally displaced people, refugees and communities affected by crisis.
Food support is more than a meal: it is stability in a time of uncertainty, dignity in a time of hardship, and hope in a time of crisis.
Beneficiaries
3,550,152 families
19,332,863 individuals
Key activities
Dry food distribution
Hot meals
Food baskets
Emergency food response
Support aligned with humanitarian food-security standards
Impact figures
776,427 tonnes of dry food distributed
14,429,226 hot meals distributed
2,450,099 food parcels distributed

--- Sector: Health and Medical Support ---

Health and Medical Support
BCF supports health services for vulnerable communities through medical projects, support to facilities, and treatment programmes.
Health care protects more than the body. It protects dignity, the stability of a family, and the right to live in hope.

--- Story section: A Legacy of Service ---

A Legacy
of Service
BCF was officially established in 2005 in Erbil, capital of the Kurdistan Region of Iraq, to turn compassion into organized humanitarian action.

--- Story section: Institutional Timeline ---

Institutional
Timeline

--- Story section: Mission ---

Mission
To provide humanitarian aid to the most vulnerable people, regardless of ethnic, religious or political background, while promoting peace, dignity and sustainability for humanity and nature.

--- Story section: Vision ---

Vision
A world where poverty and forced migration are eliminated, and where every person has access to education, essential services, basic rights and a protected environment.

--- Story section: Humanitarian Philosophy ---

Humanitarian
Philosophy
Humanitarian work is not charity from above. It is service with dignity, delivered to people as human beings, not as statistics.

--- Story section: Values Behind the Mission ---

Values
Behind the Mission
BCF operates through values that shape both local and international humanitarian work.

--- Values ---

Orphan Care Project

2011
Van Earthquake Response
BCF crossed into Türkiye to support families affected by the Van earthquake.
2013
Welcoming Syrian Refugees
By 2013, BCF had welcomed more than 250,000 Syrian refugees.
2014
9 Million Meals — Sinjar Mountain Lifeline
BCF provided 9 million hot meals to displaced families on Sinjar Mountain.
2015
Camp Management in Erbil
BCF managed camps serving displaced people and refugees in Erbil.
2016
International Recognition
UN ECOSOC Consultative Status and charity registration in Kuwait.
2018
Sphere Representation
BCF became the Sphere representative in the Kurdistan Region.
2019
Cross-Border Response in Syria
BCF crossed borders to support civilians affected by armed conflict in Syria.
2020
British Licensing & Duhok Camps
Licensed by the Charity Commission in Britain and operating camps in Duhok.
2021
First Autism Humanitarian Center
BCF opened Kurdistan's first humanitarian center dedicated to autism.
2023
ISO 9001:2015 & Earthquake Response
BCF achieved ISO 9001:2015 certification and was the first organization to reach major earthquake victims in Türkiye and Syria.
2025
Drug Rehabilitation Center
BCF laid the foundation stone for Kurdistan–Iraq's first drug rehabilitation center.

BCF delivered 465 humanitarian shipments to Syria after the armed conflict.
